Our Three Pillars

The Foundation of Green Living

Every thriving ecosystem rests on three essentials. We protect and restore all three.

Healthy Soil

Restoring Nepal's soil microbiome through bio-compost and mycorrhizal inoculants, turning degraded land into thriving, fertile ground.

Soil health metrics: +32% organic matter, 48% more microbial activity, and better moisture retention across 60+ farm plots.

Healthy Food

Growing chemical-free, nutrient-dense produce that feeds families with integrity — from seed selection to harvest, every step is traceable.

Food impact stats: 35% higher crop nutrition, 42% less chemical input, and improved local harvest yields for small farms.

Healthy Water

Protecting Nepal's glacial watersheds by eliminating chemical runoff and building organic buffer zones around rivers, lakes, and springs.

Water health stats: 60% runoff reduction, cleaner stream flow, and restored native aquatic habitat along hillside watersheds.

StartUp Entrepreneur

Baburam Niroula

Founder & CEO: GoGreen Nepal

"At GoGreen Nepal, our journey began with a simple realization i.e. healthy soil means a healthier future. Seeing the impact of chemical farming in my home district inspired us to work closely with Nepali farmers, promoting sustainable practices that protect both the land and the communities who depend on it."

Baburam Niroula founded Go Green Nepal after witnessing first-hand how chemical farming was depleting his home district's soil. Today, the company works with over 500 smallholder farmers across the Terai and Hills.
