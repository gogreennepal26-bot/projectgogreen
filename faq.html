Everything you need to know about GoGreen™

Discover how our flagship organic protection product works, why it is built for Nepal’s climate, and how farmers can join the movement toward safer, more sustainable agriculture.

What exactly is Organic Shield™, and how does it work? +

Organic Shield™ is our flagship bio-protectant prototype developed specifically to protect crops without using harsh, synthetic chemicals. Formulated from native Himalayan botanicals and backed by over 7 years of soil science research, it coats plants in a natural protective barrier. It effectively guards crops against common fungal and bacterial threats while remaining 100% safe for the beneficial microbes living in your soil.

Can Organic Shield™ really withstand the heavy Nepali monsoon season? +

Yes! We specifically engineered Organic Shield™ to be "Monsoon Ready." Traditional chemical sprays often wash away during heavy downpours, forcing farmers to reapply them constantly. Our formula is designed to bond safely with the plant's surface, ensuring it remains highly effective through intense rainy cycles and protecting your harvest when it is most vulnerable to moisture-driven diseases.

I am a local farmer. How can I join your network or get on the waitlist? +

We love working hand-in-hand with local growers! We currently partner with over 500 smallholder farmers across the Terai and Hills. You can join our waitlist by navigating to our "Innovation" section and clicking Join the Waitlist, or by filling out the contact form below with your role selected as "Farmer / Grower." Our team will reach out to discuss how we can help transition or support your farm with sustainable organic solutions.

How does Go Green Nepal ensure its products are safe for consumers? +

Our core guiding philosophy is Healthy Soil, Healthy Water, Healthy Food. Unlike synthetic pesticides that leave toxic residues on produce, Organic Shield™ is designed to naturally and completely break down before the crop ever reaches a kitchen table. This guarantees zero harvest residue, meaning families get to enjoy genuinely chemical-free, nutrient-dense food grown with total integrity.

Where is Go Green Nepal located, and can I visit your operation? +

Our main office and research operations are rooted in Biratchowk, Nepal. We are deeply proud to harness Nepal's natural soil and glacial watersheds to create localized agricultural innovations. If you are an investor, researcher, or partner looking to visit us, please drop us a message through our contact form or email us at gogreennepal26@gmail.com to schedule a visit!
